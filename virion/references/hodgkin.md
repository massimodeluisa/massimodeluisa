# Classical Hodgkin lymphoma

A test of a name that is not a particle. The skill draws one object from the public record and says what it refuses to invent.

## What Hodgkin lymphoma is

A 2025 Journal of Clinical Pathology review of the WHO fifth edition lists the minimal criteria for classic Hodgkin lymphoma: Hodgkin and Reed-Sternberg cells in a reactive microenvironment, with the immunophenotype CD30-positive, PAX5 weak to moderate, and CD20 negative, weak, or heterogeneous. That is a cell in a tissue. It is not a virion, and this figure does not draw the cell or the node.

## The object that does have a structure

CD30 is UniProt P28908, a transmembrane receptor.

- Extracellular residues 19–385.
- Transmembrane span 386–406.
- Cytoplasmic region 407–595.
- PDB 1D01 is the only experimental CD30 entry UniProt lists. Chains G, H, and I are a peptide UniProt maps to residues 576–583. RCSB deposits each chain with sequence length 9.
- The TRAF domain of TRAF2 in that entry is a C3 trimer. Biological assembly 2 is A3B3: three TRAF2 chains and three CD30 peptides. Resolution 2.00 Å. Ye et al., Molecular Cell 1999.

The stalks above the membrane are schematic. The crystal does not contain the extracellular domain. The cytoplasmic stretch from the transmembrane helix to residue 576 is not in 1D01 either, so it is a gap, not a rod. A nine-residue peptide is a stub on each TRAF domain, not a beam from the membrane. AlphaFold has a prediction for the rest of P28908. It is not drawn, and it is not labeled as an experimental structure.

NF-κB is named on the UniProt function line. It is not a part in this figure.

## Motion

The three cytoplasmic tails reach the three sites of the TRAF2 trimer. That is the complex the crystal contains. E pulls the trimer off the tails.
